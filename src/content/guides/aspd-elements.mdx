## Attack speed and its limit

**ASPD** controls how quickly you attack. **AGI** raises attack speed; **ASPD Limit** raises the cap you can reach. **Increase AGI** boosts movement speed and does not grant attack speed.

## Read the element table

Find your **attack element in the row**, then the **target’s element in the column**. The number is the percentage of damage dealt: **125%** is a bonus; **50%** halves it. Check the monster’s database entry for its element level.

## Examples of how to use elements

### Attacking a Fire monster

Find the **Water** row and the **Fire** column: a Water attack deals **125% damage**. A Fire attack against the same monster deals only **50%** at element levels 1–3, or **25%** at level 4.

### Defending against a Fire attack

Read the **Fire** row and compare the armour elements across the columns. **Fire armour** takes **50% damage** at element levels 1–3, while **Earth armour** takes **125%**. Lower percentages mean less incoming damage.

### Using Holy or Dark attacks

A **Holy** attack against an **Undead** monster deals **110% damage**. A **Dark** attack against a **Water** monster also deals **110%**. Check the matching row and column before choosing an element.
