## Start with the tutorial

Completing the tutorial is highly encouraged to gain your first levels and receive a scarf.

## Wake up as an Orphan

### 1. Speak to the Reflection

Click the **Reflection** on the left of the starting area.

<SourceScreenshot number={1} />

### 2. Choose the tutorial

Select the first option, **I want to remember (Intro and Tutorial)**.

<SourceScreenshot number={2} />

### 3. Continue with your Memories

Interact with **Memories** to enter the tutorial.

<SourceScreenshot number={3} />

### 4. Choose the second dialogue response

Choose the second dialogue option each time to receive extra experience and items.

<SourceScreenshot number={4} />

### 5. Allocate starting stats and finish

Start by increasing **STR** for early Orphan leveling. After completing the tutorial, you should have:

- Base/Job level **6/3**.
- **Toy Knife**.
- **+2 Sandals**, with **Movement Speed +5%**.
- A scarf of your choice.
- **3 Initiate Potions**.

## Choose your scarf

Choose one of these nine scarves near the end of the tutorial.

<SourceScreenshot number={5} />

<ScarfTable />

Scarves cannot be traded, but you can put them in storage. Complete the tutorial on another character if you want a different scarf.

## Collect your starting supplies

Speak to the **Nanny** in the hallway after the tutorial.

<SourceScreenshot number={6} />

She gives you **15 Initiate Potions**, **3 Initiate Butterfly Wings**, **2 Initiate Magnifiers**, an **Orphan Badge**, and a **Memory Mirror**. The initiate consumables cannot be transferred or traded.

## Keep the Orphan Badge

Use the badge to return to the Orphanage. It is **not consumed**, so you can reuse it.

<SourceScreenshot number={7} />

## Understand the Memory Mirror

Use the Memory Mirror to reset your stats before **Base Level 15**. If you keep it unused until **Base Level 15** and return it to the **Nanny**, you will receive **My First Card Album**.

<MemoryMirrorReward />

## Know your starting tools

- <SkillIcon icon="AL_WARP" /> **Warp Portal**
  - Starts at **skill level 1**.
  - Type `@memo` to view your saved locations.
  - Use `@memo 0`, `@memo 1`, or `@memo 2` to save your current location in that slot.
  - You cannot save locations in dungeons or on maps with bosses.
- <SkillIcon icon="AL_TELEPORT" /> **Teleport**
  - Starts at **skill level 1**.
- <SkillIcon icon="AL_HEAL" /> **Heal**
  - Restores more HP as your base level increases.
  - Maximum skill level: **1**.
- <SkillIcon icon="TF_HIDING" /> **Hiding**
  - Hides you for **2 seconds**.
  - Works against bosses.
  - Maximum skill level: **1**.
- <SkillIcon icon="AL_INCAGI" /> **Increase AGI**
  - Sets your movement speed to maximum for **5 seconds**.
  - Cooldown: **60 seconds**.
- <SkillIcon icon="BS_GREED" /> **Area Loot**
  - Picks up all items within **2 cells** of your character.
- <SkillIcon icon="ALL_FULL_THROTTLE" /> **Morroc's Mark**
  - Fully restores your **HP and SP** when cast.
  - Grants **all stats +10%** and **movement speed +50%** for **10 seconds**.
  - Cooldown: **60 minutes**.

## Make the Orphanage your home

Return to the Orphanage to heal, repair your equipment, use storage, and travel to other locations.

<Callout title="Help the Orphanage grow">
Donate Zeny to **Amadeus Windsor** to help level up the Orphanage. Higher levels improve its services, including healer buffs, meals, transport destinations, and shops, and reduce the cost of stat and skill resets. Check its current level and donation progress with Amadeus.
</Callout>

### Healing, storage, and resets

<NpcCard name="Lucy Guille" image="images/npcs/lucy-guille.gif" width={37} height={92}>
Heal and repair your equipment for free. Lucy also clears common status effects and stripped equipment. Speak to her for paid buffs.
</NpcCard>

<NpcCard name="Kafra Allysia" image="images/npcs/kafra-alysia.gif" width={38} height={94}>
Save your respawn point, open all three storages, refill elixirs, and travel to other locations. Storage access is free.
</NpcCard>

<NpcCard name="Memory Fountain" image="images/npcs/memory-fountain.png" width={1290} height={1219}>
Reset your stats and skills at the fountain beside the stairs. The reset cost decreases as the Orphanage levels up.
</NpcCard>

### Orphan support and dungeon travel

<NpcCard name="Alfred, the Butler" image="images/npcs/alfred.gif" width={39} height={101}>
Speak to Alfred in the main hall. He can:

- Hatch eggs.
- Change the visibility of pets, summons, headgear, and garments.
- Add or remove chat channels.
- Activate **PRD: Steady Fortunes**, a new drop system that works differently from OG. Trying it is highly encouraged.
</NpcCard>

<NpcCard name="Skia Nerius" image="images/npcs/skia-nerius.gif" width={44} height={89}>
Speak with Skia Nerius in the main hall to use the Dungeon Caravan.
</NpcCard>
