## Prerequisites

<Callout title="Before you start">

- Have your **Orphan Badge** to use the Kafra travel routes below.
- Set aside **475,000 Zeny** for adding the eyes, plus travel costs.
- You only need to complete this quest **once per account**.
</Callout>

## 1) Find the mirror shard

<p>{stops.shard.access}</p>

Interact with **Bifrost Stone Shard** and choose **“Dig the thing out”** to collect the **Bifrost Mirror Shard**. Use the same option at the four eye tombs below. The red circles on the screenshots’ minimaps mark the tomb locations.

<SourceScreenshot image={images.shard} />

## 2) Collect the Eye of Vidar

<p>{stops.vidar.access}</p>

Dig up the **Eye of Vidar**. This eye opens travel to the past.

<SourceScreenshot image={images.vidar} />

## 3) Collect the Eye of Vali

<p>{stops.vali.access}</p>

Dig up the **Eye of Vali**. This eye opens travel to the future.

<SourceScreenshot image={images.vali} />

## 4) Collect the Eye of Thor

<p>{stops.thor.access}</p>

Dig up the **Eye of Thor**. This eye opens alternate timelines and deeper distortions.

<SourceScreenshot image={images.thor} />

## 5) Collect the Eye of Balder

<p>{stops.balder.access}</p>

Dig up the **Eye of Balder**. This eye opens paradoxes and end-of-the-world distortions.

<SourceScreenshot image={images.balder} />

## 6) Reach the Sages’ Study

<p>{stops.study.access}</p>

<SourceScreenshot image={images.ferry} />

<SourceScreenshot image={images.palace} />

Go past reception to the **Main Elevator** at the back. Choose **Main Corridor**.

<SourceScreenshot image={images.mainElevator} />

Head right along the corridor to the **Study Elevator**. Choose **Sage’s Study** to reach the four Masters.

<SourceScreenshot image={images.studyElevator} />

## 7) Add the eyes in order

<Callout title="Add one eye at a time" tone="warning">
Speak with the Masters in this order: **Vidar → Vali → Thor → Balder**. Each Master adds the matching eye to your mirror for the listed fee. Confirm the request to add the stone before moving to the next Master.
</Callout>

Keep the **Bifrost Mirror Shard** and all **four eyes** in your inventory while speaking with the Masters.

## 8) Rewards and distortion access

<Callout title="Quest rewards" tone="tip">

Adding the final eye grants:

- The **Complete Mirror** achievement.
- **Costume Cold Angel Wings**.
- A completed mirror that opens distortions requiring any of the four eyes.

</Callout>
