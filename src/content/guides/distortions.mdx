## Before entering a distortion

<Callout title="Before you enter">

Bring a powered-up <strong><a href={guideHref('bifrost-mirror')}>Bifrost Mirror</a></strong>. Speak with the required Master in **Dicastes Diel (Sage’s Palace)** before using the corresponding distortion. The detailed routes include these mirror unlocks; warning boxes highlight extra conditions such as an item payment or a level requirement.

The mirror’s Eyes correspond to **Vidar** (past), **Vali** (future), **Thor** (alternate timelines or deeper distortions), and **Balder** (paradoxes). Distortions disable teleportation and `/memo`; prepare before entering. Use the screenshots to find the orb and read its entry dialogue.

</Callout>
