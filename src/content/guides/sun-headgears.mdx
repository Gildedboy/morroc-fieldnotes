## Prerequisites

<Callout title="Before you start, you will need">

- Bring the **matching Moon headgear refined to exactly +9**.
- Collect **25 Jewel Elunium** for the headgear upgrade.
- Collect **15 Jewel Bradium**, **1,000 Star Pieces**, and **100 Galactic Dust** for Sun of Serpens.
- Complete the Serpens steps on the character that will craft the headgear: **Moon of Serpens and Sun of Serpens are character-bound**.

</Callout>

## 1) Get your Moon headgear and materials

<p>{dungeonEntrances.find(entrance => entrance.id === 'observatorium-astrologika')?.directions} See <a href={`${guideHref('dungeons')}#observatorium-astrologika`}>Observatorium Astrologika’s entrance</a> for the route and screenshots.</p>

Defeat **<a href="https://rtm-database.pages.dev/index.html?tab=mobs&id=2668" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer">Moon Guardian</a>** in **Observatorium Study**, the first floor of Observatorium Astrologika, to obtain a <a href="https://rtm-database.pages.dev/?q=of+the+moon" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer">Moon headgear</a>. Choose the one you want to upgrade and refine it to **exactly +9**.

Collect **<a href="https://rtm-database.pages.dev/index.html?id=6468" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer">Star Pieces</a>** and **<a href="https://rtm-database.pages.dev/index.html?id=6467" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer">Galactic Dust</a>** from the other monsters in the observatorium. You will also need **15 <a href="https://rtm-database.pages.dev/index.html?id=6226" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer">Jewel Bradium</a>** to craft Sun of Serpens.

## 2) Read the bookshelf

On the **first floor**, interact with the **Bookshelf** on the east side to begin the quest.

<SourceScreenshot image={images.bookshelf} />
<SourceScreenshot image={images.bookshelfMap} />

## 3) Visit both telescopes

Go to the **second floor**, Observatorium Window, and interact with the **Low-Range Telescope**. The red circle on the minimap marks its location.

<SourceScreenshot image={images.lowTelescope} />
<SourceScreenshot image={images.lowTelescopeMap} />

Continue to the **third floor**, Observatorium Frozen Tide, and interact with the **High-Range Telescope** at the end of the floor.

<SourceScreenshot image={images.highTelescope} />
<SourceScreenshot image={images.highTelescopeMap} />

## 4) Collect Moon of Serpens

<p>{dungeonEntrances.find(entrance => entrance.id === 'airship-crash-site')?.route?.[0]} Follow the <a href={`${guideHref('dungeons')}#airship-crash-site`}>Airship Crash Site route</a> and enter <strong>Crash Site Cave</strong> (<code>resc_dun02</code>).</p>

Find the **Stone** near the **north edge** of the cave. Interact with it to receive **Moon of Serpens**.

<SourceScreenshot image={images.stone} />
<SourceScreenshot image={images.stoneMap} />

## 5) Craft Sun of Serpens

Return to the **third floor of Observatorium Astrologika** and speak with **Frozen Tides**. Hand over:

- **1 Moon of Serpens**.
- **15 Jewel Bradium**.
- **1,000 Star Pieces**.
- **100 Galactic Dust**.

You receive **Sun of Serpens**.

<SourceScreenshot image={images.frozenTides} />
<SourceScreenshot image={images.serpens} />

## 6) Visit the matching observer

Take your **exactly +9 Moon headgear**, **25 <a href="https://rtm-database.pages.dev/index.html?id=6290" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer">Jewel Elunium</a>**, and **1 <a href="https://rtm-database.pages.dev/index.html?id=25501" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer">Sun of Serpens</a>** to its matching observer.

Use the coordinates and the white player arrow on each minimap to locate the observer.

<SunHeadgearObservers />

## 7) Rewards

<Callout title="Crafting reward" tone="tip">

- The **<a href="https://rtm-database.pages.dev/?q=of+the+sun" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer">Sun headgear</a>** matching your chosen Moon headgear.
- **Two card slots**.
- **Base Level 110** required to equip the Sun headgear.

</Callout>
