## Prerequisites

<Callout title="Before you start, you will need">

- Complete **all 90 HATred quests**.
- Prepare **Inferno Tome**, **Purgatorio Tome**, and **Paradiso Tome** using the recipes below.
- Collect **500 <a href="https://rtm-database.pages.dev/index.html?id=7361" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer">Distortion Essence</a>**, **25 Jewel Shadowdecon**, **25 Growth Shadow Coffers**, and **10 MVP Shards** for the final exchange.
- Bring your **Bifrost Mirror** for the distortion routes.

</Callout>

## 1) Complete all 90 HATred quests

Finish the full collection of **90 HATred quests**. Use the <a href="https://hatredguide.segasunset.workers.dev/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer">HATred guide</a> for each headgear’s materials, NPC location, and minimap.

## 2) Craft the three great Tomes

<p>{bloomingRoseSandalsLocation.mirrorAccess} {locations.crafting}</p>

Each Tome needs its listed manuals and jewels, plus **1,000 <a href="https://rtm-database.pages.dev/index.html?id=7360" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer">Distortion Shards</a>** and **100 <a href="https://rtm-database.pages.dev/index.html?id=7361" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer">Distortion Essence</a>**. Open a manual’s database entry for its monster drops.

## 3) Speak with Chaos and Iris

<p>{locations.payon} Follow the <a href={`${guideHref('distortions')}#old-payon`}>Old Payon entrance route</a> through Sunken Temple to reach the distortion.</p>

## 4) Enter the Coffin’s room

<p>Follow the <a href={`${guideHref('dungeons')}#death-castle`}>Death Castle route</a>. {locations.coffin}</p>

Speak with **Balder** inside and **select every available dialogue option** to hear the full story.

## 5) Return to Iris

<p>{locations.iris} Follow the conversation and choose <strong>yes</strong>.</p>

## 6) Hand over the final materials

Return to **Death’s Castle** and interact with the **Coffin** again. Speak with **Balder** and hand over:

- **1 Inferno Tome**.
- **1 Purgatorio Tome**.
- **1 Paradiso Tome**.
- **500 <a href="https://rtm-database.pages.dev/index.html?id=7361" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer">Distortion Essence</a>**.
- **25 <a href="https://rtm-database.pages.dev/index.html?id=23080" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer">Jewel Shadowdecon</a>**.
- **25 <a href="https://rtm-database.pages.dev/index.html?id=14735" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer">Growth Shadow Coffers</a>**.
- **10 <a href="https://rtm-database.pages.dev/index.html?id=7615" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer">MVP Shards</a>**.

## 7) Rewards

<SourceScreenshot image={{ src: 'images/celestial-tome/celestial-tome.png', width: 75, height: 100, alt: 'Celestial Tome item artwork from the Refuge database' }} hideCaption />

<Callout title="Quest reward" tone="tip">

- **<a href="https://rtm-database.pages.dev/index.html?id=24236" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer">Celestial Tome</a>** from **Balder** — account-bound.
- **Base Level 130** required to equip it.
- **All Stats +2**, **Movement Speed +5%**, **HIT +15**, and **Total HIT +5%**.
- Healing and movement skill bonuses, including **Coluceo Heal Lv. 2**, **Resurrection Lv. 4**, and **Convenio Lv. 1**.

</Callout>
