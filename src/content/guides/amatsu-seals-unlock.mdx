Awaken **all four seals** to grant **20% additional EXP server-wide for one hour** and **weaken the entire Amatsu SS distortion dungeon**.
</Callout>

## Reach Tatami Maze

{locations.find(location => location.id === 'amatsu-dungeon')?.access}

Enter **Tatami Maze**. See the <a href={`${guideHref('dungeons')}#amatsu-dungeon`}>Amatsu Dungeon entrance screenshots</a> for the approach.

## Seal route

Start at **START** in the southeast corner and visit **I → II → III → IV / END**.

<SourceScreenshot image={images.route} caption="Tatami Maze · follow the colored paths between the four numbered seals." />

## Activate and clear each seal

<Callout title="Before attempting" tone="warning">
All summoned enemies are **aggressive** and can be difficult for players leveling in this area. Attempt the seals **with a party or help from a high-level player**.

If someone else awakens the seals for the entire server, remember to **thank them**!
</Callout>

Speak to the small stone and choose **Touch the stone** when ready to fight. Defeat **all enemies it summons within 90 seconds**. Watch the chat counter for enemies still standing and the message that the seal wakes beneath Amatsu.

<SourceScreenshot image={images['seal-dialogue']} caption="Seal activation dialogue." />

A sealed stone is small. Once cleared, it becomes a taller, jagged stone and its name gains **[awake]**. Check this state before moving to the next stop.
