## Choose monsters by level

Refuge gives full EXP for monsters from **3 levels below through 5 levels above** your Base Level. Monsters **6–15 levels above** give increased EXP, up to 120% at +15; monsters **4–9 levels below** give reduced EXP. Monsters **16 or more levels above** or **10 or more levels below** give only 1% EXP. See [Refuge’s experience rules](https://rtm-database.pages.dev/leveling).

The ranges below are **monster levels**, not recommended character levels. “EXP starts” uses the lowest monster level: subtract 15 to find the first Base Level where that monster is outside the 1% EXP penalty, with Base Lv. 1 as the minimum. The ranges use current Refuge monster records, including MVPs where listed. See [Refuge’s place and monster records](https://rtm-database.pages.dev/places).

Locations are ordered by lowest monster level, then highest.
