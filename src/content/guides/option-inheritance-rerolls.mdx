Your old gear keeps its rolls. If you reroll a line, it draws from the updated pool. The other lines stay as they are.

<Callout title="Items you cannot recycle" tone="warning">
- Equipped items, including those in your equipment-switch setup.
- Favorites, bound items, and rentals.
- Items with cards or enchants.
- Class Gems, costumes, and items without random options.
</Callout>

<Callout title="Recycling consumes the selected equipment" tone="warning">
Choose the pieces and confirm the exchange. They are permanently destroyed, including their refines and rolls.
</Callout>

## Inherit

<Callout title="One line at a time">
Copy one existing random-option line from a matching donor item with **100% success**. It cannot add lines: a three-line item cannot gain a fourth line through inheritance.
</Callout>

1. **Equip the item you want to keep.** Bring a donor copy of the **exact same item** with a compatible option pool.
2. **Choose the line to replace.** Croc Alt-Eisen copies the donor’s option and value onto the same line of your item. For example, **ATK% on the donor’s first line will replace MATK% on your item’s first line**.
3. **Pay the transfer cost** listed below. The donor is permanently consumed.

<Callout title="Your equipped item">
Will keep its refine, cards, enchants, binding, and all the other lines.
</Callout>

<Callout title="Donor requirements" tone="warning">
- Must be unbound, with no cards or enchants. Trial and rental items are not accepted.
- Must be unequipped.
- Cannot be in your equipment-switch setup.
- Cannot be marked as a favorite.
</Callout>

## Reroll

<Callout title="One line at a time">
Replace one random-option line with **100% success**. No donor is needed.
</Callout>

1. **Equip the item you want to reroll.**
2. **Choose the line to replace.** Croc Alt-Eisen rolls a new option and value for that line. The same attribute cannot repeat: an ATK% line will roll a different attribute, rather than another ATK% value.
3. **Pay the reroll cost** listed below and keep the result.

<Callout title="Your equipped item">
Will keep its refine, cards, enchants, binding, and all the other lines.
</Callout>

<Callout title="Before you reroll" tone="warning">
- The new roll can be weaker.
- Every attempt costs materials and Zeny.
- There is no preview or undo.
- A line needs another available attribute to be rerolled. Otherwise, it will not be offered or charged.
- An empty line can only be filled if the item’s current pool supports it.
- Manuals and Runes cannot be rerolled, even without engravings.
- Blooming Rose Sandals cannot be rerolled because their completion options are fixed.
</Callout>

## Restrictions

<Callout title="Items Croc Alt-Eisen cannot modify" tone="danger">
- Items with **Amatsu seals or Valhalla words** cannot use inheritance or rerolls.
- If Croc Alt-Eisen cannot identify an item’s option pool, he refuses the service.
</Callout>
