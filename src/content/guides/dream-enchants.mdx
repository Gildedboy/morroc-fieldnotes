## Prerequisites

<Callout title="Before you start, you will need">

- Equipment whose description allows a **Dream Enchant**.
- **100 Dream Shards** for a random enchant, or **2,000 Dream Shards** to choose the result with guaranteed success.
- To remove an existing enchant: **500 Distortion Shards** or **100 Distortion Essence**.

</Callout>

## 1) Choose eligible equipment

Read the equipment description for Dream Enchant availability. This system applies to eligible gear, including some Sky Garden rewards, Rachel distortion gear, and Judge weapons.

The random enchant’s **success chance depends on the item’s refine level**. At **+10**, Dream Enchants unlock the additional effects shown in the table below.

## 2) Gather Dream Shards

Bring **<a href="https://rtm-database.pages.dev/index.html?id=7364" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer">Dream Shards</a>** to pay for the enchantment. You can obtain them from **<a href="https://rtm-database.pages.dev/index.html?id=7365" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer">Dream Pouches</a>**.

The **Dream Box** in the Orphanage exchanges **100 <a href="https://rtm-database.pages.dev/index.html?id=7361" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer">Distortion Essence</a>** for **1 Dream Pouch**. Open the pouch to receive Dream Shards.

## 3) Speak with the Weaver of Dreams

## 4) Apply or remove the enchant

Choose **Enchant** for a random result, or **Guaranteed Enchant — choose result** to select the bonus you want. Select the eligible item and confirm the exchange.

To replace an existing enchant, use **Remove Enchant**, choose one of its two payment options, then enchant the item again.

<Callout title="Dream Enchants and random rolls">
Removing a Dream Enchant does not reroll the equipment’s <a href={guideHref('random-rolls')}>random options</a>.
</Callout>

## 5) Compare the enchant bonuses

The **At refine +10** column shows the extra effect unlocked when the enchanted equipment reaches +10. Check any base-stat condition in the last column as well.
