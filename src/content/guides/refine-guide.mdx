## Before you refine

<Callout title="Refining rules">

- The maximum refine level is **+10**.
- A failed attempt **does not destroy the item**. It can lower its refine level by **1**.
- Higher-tier ores can protect against downgrades on lower tiers, at **twice the Zeny cost**.

</Callout>

## 1) Visit Heimdall Alt-Eisen to start refining

<NpcCard name="Heimdall Alt-Eisen" image="images/npcs/heimdall.gif" width={74} height={117}>
Find Heimdall in the **Orphanage smithy**, at the **Refining and Ores** service. Bring the equipment, matching ore, and Zeny.
</NpcCard>

Drag the item from your inventory into the refining window, then select a refining material. The window displays the success chance and Zeny fee for the attempt.

<SourceScreenshot image={{ src: 'images/refining/refining-136.jpg', width: 1904, height: 1041, alt: 'Refining window with Moonlight Dagger selected and material choices above it', crop: { x: 816, y: 346, width: 270, height: 350 } }} />

## 2) Choose the correct material

The columns show the refine level you are trying to reach.

<RefiningMaterials />

### Class Gems

**Class Gems** have their own material requirements:

- **Normal:** failure loses one refine level, down to +0.
- **Protected:** failure keeps the refine level. It costs more materials and Zeny, with the **same success chance**.

<ClassGemRefining />

## 3) Buy or exchange materials

### Howard

Buy ores from **Howard**. Higher **Orphanage levels** unlock more materials in his shop.

<SourceScreenshot image={{ src: 'images/refining/howard-shop.jpg', width: 1904, height: 1041, alt: 'Howard’s shop lists Oridecon and Elunium for 1,000 Zeny each, HD ores for 5,000 each, and Enriched ores for 15,000 each', crop: { x: 0, y: 390, width: 280, height: 312 } }} />

### Croc Alt-Eisen

Speak with **Croc Alt-Eisen** in the **Orphanage smithy** to exchange lower-tier refining materials for higher-tier ones.

<CrocExchange />

### Shadow Assistant

Trade <a href="https://rtm-database.pages.dev/index.html?id=25728" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer">Shadow Ore</a> with the **Shadow Assistant** in the smithy for **Jewel Shadowdecon**, **Elunium**, or **Oridecon**.

<ShadowAssistantExchange />

## 4) Use downgrade protection

To prevent weapon or armour downgrades, use **HD ores for +1–+3**, **Enriched ores for +4–+6**, or **Jewels for +7–+9**. Protected attempts cost **twice as much Zeny**.

**Jewel Bradium** and **Jewel Shadowdecon** can protect their matching items from downgrading through **+9**.

<Callout title="Protection does not guarantee success">
A protected attempt can still fail. The protection prevents the downgrade.
</Callout>
