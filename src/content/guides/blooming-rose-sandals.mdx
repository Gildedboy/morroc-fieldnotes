## Prerequisites

<Callout title="Before you start">

- Collect **500 Distortion Shards**.
- Collect **10 MVP Shards**.
- Bring **1 pair of Old Rose Sandals refined to exactly +7**.
- Have your **Bifrost Mirror** for the distortion route.

</Callout>

## 1) Obtain and refine Old Rose Sandals

Get **1 pair of <a href="https://rtm-database.pages.dev/index.html?id=22070" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer">Old Rose Sandals</a>** from one of these monsters:

- **Lost Traveler** — <a href="https://rtm-database.pages.dev/places?id=mansion-garden" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer">Mansion Garden</a>.
- **Navigator** — <a href="https://rtm-database.pages.dev/places?id=mansion-hidden-garden" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer">Mansion Hidden Garden</a>.
- **Paradox Amdarais (MVP)** — <a href="https://rtm-database.pages.dev/places?id=mansion-lost-area" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer">Mansion Lost Area</a>.

Refine the **Old Rose Sandals to exactly +7** before taking them to Rose Sage.

<Callout title="Random rolls">
Old Rose Sandals can have random roll options.
</Callout>

<SourceScreenshot image={images.oldSandals} />

## 2) Collect the required materials

Gather **500 <a href="https://rtm-database.pages.dev/index.html?id=7360" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer">Distortion Shards</a>** from monsters in <a href={guideHref('distortions')}>distortion dungeons</a>.

Gather **10 <a href="https://rtm-database.pages.dev/index.html?id=7615" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer">MVP Shards</a>** from **MVP Prize Boxes** obtained from MVPs.

## 3) Visit Rose Sage in Varmundt’s Mansion

<p>{location.mirrorAccess} See the <a href={guideHref('bifrost-mirror')}>Bifrost Mirror guide</a> if you need to unlock the mirror route.</p>

<SourceScreenshot image={images.orb} />

Find **Rose Sage** in the **northeast part of Varmundt’s Mansion — Interior**. Look for the **Rose Sandals Repair** service label.

<SourceScreenshot image={images.sage} />

Speak with Rose Sage. Choose **“What do you need to fix them?”** to view the required materials.

<SourceScreenshot image={images.dialogue} />

<SourceScreenshot image={images.materials} />

Choose **“Please, fix my Sandals!”** and hand over the materials.

## 4) Rewards

<Callout title="Crafting reward" tone="tip">

- **Blooming Rose Sandals [1]**.
- Blooming Rose Sandals will always have **fixed rolls**.
- **Base Level 120** required to equip them.
- **Movement Speed +10%** and **ASPD Limit +1**.
- Refine bonuses activate when the matching base stat reaches **90**.

</Callout>

<SourceScreenshot image={images.reward} />

Check the <a href="https://rtm-database.pages.dev/index.html?id=22071" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer">Blooming Rose Sandals database entry</a> for the full stat and refine bonuses.
